# Oventric Animated Hero — Hybrid Showcase

## Goal
Create an original, cinematic homepage hero inspired by G2A’s energy without copying its gaming artwork. Oventric’s version will immediately communicate a digital marketplace and creator community.

## Creative direction
- Keep the existing headline, Shop, Become a Seller, and search actions readable on the left.
- Replace the static right-side image with an 8–10 second seamless visual loop.
- Show one creator at a modern workstation while layered digital-product covers, storefront tiles, social posts, chat activity, and earnings cues move through the scene.
- Use Oventric’s crimson with cyan, yellow, and violet accents from the current homepage.
- Preserve the soft white fade behind the text so the hero remains accessible and calm despite the motion.

## Storyboard
1. A creator works at a laptop in a polished studio setting.
2. Digital asset cards emerge from the screen: templates, design packs, software tools, audio, and services.
3. The cards sweep into a lively marketplace arrangement with subtle buyer and community activity.
4. A sale confirmation and creator earnings moment appears without showing invented prices or financial claims.
5. The scene resolves back to the opening composition for a smooth loop.

## Website integration
- Use a muted, autoplaying, looping hero video with inline playback.
- Keep the current static hero image as the poster and fallback.
- Add a pause/play control for accessibility.
- Respect reduced-motion and data-saving preferences by showing only the static image.
- Load the video after the main page content and use mobile-optimized video sizing to protect page speed.
- Keep the existing hero copy, buttons, search, mobile layout, and all sections below unchanged.

## Technical details
- Produce a 16:9 MP4 hero loop, with the important subject framed toward the right so left-side text stays clear.
- Compress and inspect the final asset before adding it to the project.
- Add the video only to the homepage hero and verify desktop and mobile layouts.
- Confirm no overlap, readable contrast, smooth playback, static fallback, and no browser errors.
