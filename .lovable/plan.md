# Newsfeed navigation refresh

## What will change
- Remove the search field and old All / Posts / Photos & Video / Assets / People filters below the composer.
- Add a search icon before Notifications in the compact mobile/tablet header and the desktop toolbar.
- Remove the desktop “Newsfeed” label beside Back to Home.
- Add a horizontally scrollable, category-inspired tab row: For You, Following, Creators, Shops, All.

## Tab content
- **For You:** Rank posts using the viewer’s existing reactions, saves, comments, and viewed popularity signals; mix in marketplace products, but exclude creator suggestion cards.
- **Following:** Show posts from both people the viewer follows and people who follow the viewer.
- **Creators:** Show the requested “No creators content found” state until its content is defined later.
- **Shops:** Show marketplace products in scrollable Shop the Feed rails, linking to product pages.
- **All:** Show the complete chronological post feed with the existing people and shop sections.

## Visual treatment
- Use compact tinted icon tabs based on the homepage category cards, with mixed blue, violet, green, amber, and teal accents.
- Preserve the current sticky hide-on-scroll behavior, composer, post actions, search results, product cards, and mobile layout.

## Validation
- Check desktop and mobile layouts, tab switching, search opening, following/follower filtering, empty Creators state, and product links.
